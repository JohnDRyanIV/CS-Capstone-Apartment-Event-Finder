Born from a real need.

As undergraduate students approaching graduation, Lawton, John, and Elisabeth kept running into the same wall: there was no single, honest resource for people just starting to make their way in the world.

Signing a lease, calculating what you could actually afford, figuring out how to build a social life from scratch in a brand-new city — it was all scattered, confusing, and weirdly hard to find good answers to.

So they built Pocket Movers. Not as a class project, but as something they genuinely needed — and knew others did too.
